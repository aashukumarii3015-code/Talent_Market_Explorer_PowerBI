# Talent Market Explorer 📊

### Candidate, Role & Salary Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

Talent Market Explorer is an interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes candidate profiles, job-search behavior, employment status, interview activity, and salary expectations to uncover meaningful talent market insights.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze candidate profiles and employment status.
* Explore candidate distribution across job sectors.
* Understand job-search channels and candidate availability.
* Compare current and expected salaries.
* Provide data-driven recruitment and compensation recommendations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* DAX
* Data Analysis
* Data Visualization

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Talent Market Overview

* Overview of the candidate pool.
* Candidate distribution across job sectors.
* Summary of current and expected salaries.

### 2. Candidate & Role Analysis

* Employment status and notice period analysis.
* Job-search channel analysis.
* Education and interview activity insights.

### 3. Salary Intelligence

* Highest current and expected salaries.
* Salary comparisons across sectors and location types.
* Salary analysis by experience.

### 4. Key Insights & Business Recommendations

* Summary of important findings.
* Candidate availability and recruitment channel insights.
* Recommendations for recruitment and compensation planning.

## 🔑 Key Insights

* IT & Software has the largest candidate representation.
* LinkedIn and Naukri/Indeed are the leading job-search channels in the dataset.
* Short notice periods may indicate greater candidate availability.
* Expected salaries are higher than current salaries overall.
* Salary levels vary across sectors and location types.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Prioritize suitable candidates based on availability and role requirements.
* Focus recruitment efforts on popular job-search platforms.
* Compare current and expected salaries when planning compensation.
* Use candidate and salary insights to support recruitment decisions.

## 📈 Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform candidate and salary data into actionable insights for talent analysis, recruitment planning, and compensation decisions.
